Aura+

A native macOS and iOS companion for your Oura Ring data.

Aura+ reads your own Oura Ring measurements through the official Oura Cloud API and presents them with deeper analysis and clearer visualisation than a plain daily score: hypnograms, personal baselines, lagged correlations, illness early-warning signals and long-term trends.

What it does

  • Sleep architecture — real hypnograms from 5-minute sleep-phase data, overnight heart rate and HRV curves, sleep regularity and social jetlag.
  • Score drivers — decomposes today's readiness against each contributor's own 28-day baseline, so a low score has a named cause.
  • Correlations — lagged relationships between tags, workouts and the following day's recovery, always reported with sample size.
  • Anomaly watch — combined body-temperature, resting heart rate and HRV deviation as an early signal.
  • Trends and load — long-range trend lines, personal records, and acute-to-chronic training load.
  • Widgets — macOS menu bar and Notification Center, iOS Home Screen, Lock Screen and StandBy.

Where your data lives

On your device. Aura+ has no backend: it talks to the Oura API directly from your Mac or iPhone, and stores what it fetches locally. There is no Aura+ server that receives, relays or retains your health data. See the privacy policy for the full picture.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 August 2026

Aura+ is a local-first application. It has no backend service. Your health data is read from the Oura Cloud API directly by the app running on your own Mac or iPhone, and is stored on that device. No Aura+ server receives, relays, stores or processes it, because no such server exists.

What data the app accesses

With your explicit authorisation, Aura+ reads the following from your Oura account:

  • Daily sleep, readiness, activity, stress, resilience and SpO2 summaries
  • Detailed sleep periods, including sleep stages, heart rate and heart-rate variability
  • Heart-rate time series and ring battery level
  • Workouts and guided or unguided sessions
  • Tags you have entered, and rest-mode periods
  • Cardiovascular age and VO2 max
  • Ring configuration (model, size, firmware version)
  • Basic profile attributes: age, height, weight, biological sex

Aura+ does not request the email scope and does not read your email address. You choose which scopes to grant when you authorise the app, and you may revoke access at any time from your Oura account.

Where it is stored

  • Health data is written to the app's own container on your device.
  • Access credentials are stored in the system Keychain, never in plain files.
  • Nothing is uploaded to any Aura+ infrastructure.
  • If you enable iCloud backup for your device, your device backup may include this data under Apple's terms; Aura+ does not operate that backup.

Optional AI features

Aura+ can describe its findings in plain language. Two separate mechanisms exist, and they differ in where processing happens:

  • On-device (default). Apple's on-device language model runs locally. Nothing leaves your device.
  • Anthropic API (off by default, opt-in). If you supply your own Anthropic API key and explicitly enable this feature, Aura+ sends derived, aggregated statistics — such as averages, deviations from baseline and correlation coefficients — to Anthropic's API to be turned into written commentary. Raw time series and identifying information are not sent. Before each request the app shows you exactly what would be transmitted. Anthropic's handling of that data is governed by their own terms. Disabling the feature stops all such transmission.

What Aura+ does not do

  • No analytics, telemetry or usage tracking
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
  • No sale or sharing of personal data with third parties
  • No account, registration or profile on our side

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes its local data. You can also clear stored data from within the app's settings, and revoke Aura+'s access to your Oura account at any time from your Oura account settings — after which the app can no longer read anything new.

Children

Aura+ is not directed at children under 13 and is not intended for their use.
